# Business Model & Platform Monetization

DBC operates a hybrid B2B/B2C marketplace model connecting property owners (Customers) with verified builders, contractors, and design consultants (Providers).

## 1. Opportunities & Leads Sourcing Channels

### A. Project Requests (Private Booking)
* **Flow:** Customer discovers a specific professional profile on the marketplace and submits a private request directly to them.
* **Data backing:** Backed by the `Booking` entity.
* **Workflow:** Private negotiation ➔ Direct quote ➔ Project start.

### B. Leads (Marketplace General Posting)
* **Flow:** Customer posts general construction requirements to the public board.
* **Data backing:** Backed by the `Requirement` entity.
* **Workflow:** Open bidding ➔ Quotations comparison ➔ Selection ➔ Project start.

## 2. Monetization Mechanisms

### A. Subscription Passes (Implemented)
Customers and providers explore subscription plans to unlock additional benefits.
* **Customer Pro Pass:** E.g., "Abhista Gold Package" allows viewing provider contact phone numbers and booking direct consultation schedules.

### B. Project Commission / Brokerage (Planned)
* Platform commission percentage deducted from project milestone payouts.
