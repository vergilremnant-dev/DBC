# Product Vision

The long-term vision of DesignBuildConnect is to establish a secure, escrow-backed, end-to-end management network for the residential and commercial construction industry.

## Vision Pillars
1. **Zero Booking Uncertainty:** Transition from on-demand slots to clear milestone-based project quotes.
2. **Financial Protection:** Escrow-held milestone payments released only upon joint customer and professional progress approvals.
3. **Cross-Disciplinary Workspaces:** A unified project dashboard where architects, civil engineers, and manual trade professionals coordinate construction stages under a single project sheet.
