# Project Overview: DesignBuildConnect (DBC)

DesignBuildConnect (DBC) is a premium digital matching and milestone management platform tailored for long-term construction, masonry, interior design, and architectural services.

Unlike traditional on-demand "instant-gig" applications, DBC is designed around the lifecycle of complex, high-budget, milestone-based civil engineering and home development projects.

## Core Marketplace Objective
* **Trust Verification:** Verify trade partners, contractors, and design consultants to eliminate construction matching hazards.
* **Transparent Quotes:** Enable customers to post construction requirements and compare structured, milestone-based quotation worksheets.
* **Secure Execution:** Track active project phases, resource assignments, and payments securely.
